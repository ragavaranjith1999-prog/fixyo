Washing Machine Repair Questions

Drum spinning failure commonly stems from a snapped or slipped drive belt, worn motor carbon brushes, a faulty door lock switch, a jammed drain pump preventing water evacuation, or a motor capacitor issue. Our diagnostic identifies the exact fault.
Extreme vibration or banging noises during high spin cycles are typically caused by worn shock absorbers, broken suspension springs, an unbalanced load, or worn tub bearings. We inspect and replace suspension components at your home.
Hard water minerals, lint, and residual detergent coagulate on the outer surface of the spin tub over time, causing stubborn odors and transferring grime to freshly washed clothes. Our ₹799 deep clean dismantles the drum for complete descaling and sanitization.
