About Fixyo – Home Appliance Service Center

Delivering honest, skilled, and transparent doorstep appliance repairs, residential electrical work, and plumbing solutions to families across Madurai.

Our Story

Who We Are

Fixyo is a professional home appliance service center operating from Krishnapuram Colony in Madurai, Tamil Nadu. Modern households depend heavily on air conditioners, refrigerators, and washing machines for their daily comfort and well-being. When an essential appliance breaks down, finding a dependable technician with fair pricing and verified technical expertise is often a stressful challenge.

Fixyo was established to bridge this gap. We provide a dependable, organized doorstep service model that replaces opaque quotes and unreliable scheduling with published baseline prices, qualified technicians, and instant WhatsApp booking coordination.

Our Mission

To provide Madurai households with prompt, transparent, and skilled doorstep appliance repair and home maintenance services, ensuring peace of mind through honest communication and quality diagnostics.

Our Vision

To be Madurai’s most trusted home service brand, known for technical competence, customer respect, and transparent service ethics.

Our Service Philosophy

We hold three non-negotiable principles in every service we deliver:

  • Accurate Diagnosis: We test with multimeters and pressure gauges rather than guessing, fixing the true root cause of failure.
  • Transparent Costing: Customers receive an honest breakdown of repair requirements before any replacement part is fitted.
  • Respect for Home: Our technicians carry protective mats, handle equipment carefully, and leave your workspace clean after testing.

What We Service

Air Conditioners

Split & window AC general service, foam wash, gas charging, and precision wall installations.

Refrigerators

Single door, double door, and inverter fridge cooling diagnostics, relays, and original gas filling.

Washing Machines

Front load and top load spin failure, motor belt checks, drain blockage clearance, and deep drum descaling.

Electrical & Plumbing

Residential switchboard wiring, short-circuit tracking, MCB fixes, tap leak repairs, and pipe unblocking.
