Service Standard

Our 4-Stage AC Service Protocol

Every AC booking follows a strict diagnostic and testing routine to guarantee cooling efficiency.

1

Multi-Point Diagnostic

We measure amp draw, thermostat calibration, PCB error history, and refrigerant pressure.

2

Coil & Blower Cleansing

High-pressure wash removes embedded dust, molds, and blockage without bending cooling fins.

3

Gas & Electrical Check

Joint integrity is pressure-tested with nitrogen and wiring terminals are retightened.

4

Cooling Verification

Air-throw temperature is digitally logged to confirm fast, crisp room cooling before completion.
